Trading Desk · five looks
Static sketches of the same Desk page in five styles. Numbers are placeholders. Nothing here is in the app yet.

1 · Riverfront

A dusk river scene behind everything: minaret skyline and palms on the far bank, water that moves, a sailboat, a small motorboat and a rowing shell drifting by. Widgets are frosted glass in the scene's colours (river teal, dusk navy, sunset gold, coral). The scene could sit behind Home and widgets, and stay still on the chart page.

2 · Ember glass

What you have now, taken further: warm glow behind soft translucent cards, rounder corners, bigger numbers, more space. The smallest change from today.

3 · Bento

Rounded tiles of different sizes; the most important tile on each page gets a solid colour and a big headline number. Friendly and very scannable, less "terminal".

4 · Aurora

Deep navy with a slow moving aurora (teal and violet) behind dark glass. Cooler and more mysterious than Ember; movement is in the background only.

5 · Mono

Pure black, square corners, monospace everything, one acid accent. The densest and fastest to read; no decoration at all.
